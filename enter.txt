Characteristics of Business :
Group 1, Business kya hai: Economic Activity, Production or Exchange, Sale or Transfer for Value
Group 2, Business kaise chalta hai: Regular and Continuous, Organized and Systematic, Use of Resources and Technology
Group 3, Maqsad aur khatra: Profit Motive, Risk and Uncertainty, Customer Satisfaction
Group 4, Mahol aur usool: Social Responsibility, Lawful and Ethical, Dynamic and Competitive

Objectives of Business (Business ke maqasid) :
Qisam	Points
Group 1, Economic Objectives (paisa aur karobari taraqqi)	Profit Earning, Survival and Stability, Growth and Expansion, Customer Satisfaction, Innovation and Improvement, Optimum Use of Resources, Fair Return to Owners, Market Leadership
Group 2, Social Objectives (muashre ke liye)	Employment Generation, Social Responsibility, Compliance with Law and Ethics
Group 3, Human Objectives (insanon ke liye) Welfare of Employees

Role of Business in Economic Progress of the Country :
Group 1, Paida-war aur aamdani: Production, Employment, National Income, Capital Formation
Group 2, Tijarat: Domestic Trade, Foreign Trade
Group 3, Taraqqi ke zariye: Technology, Government Revenue, Infrastructure
Group 4, Muashre aur istehkam: SMEs, Regional Development, Living Standards, Entrepreneurship, Social Welfare, Economic Stability
